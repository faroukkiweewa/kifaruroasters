Quality Seeds & Varieties

We provide certified disease-free seeds with high germination rates and locally adapted genetics. Our varieties include improved Arabica hybrids, high-yield Robusta clones, disease-resistant varieties, and climate-resilient seedlings tailored to Uganda's diverse growing conditions.

Apply for Seeds

Training & Best Practices

We promote sustainable farming through comprehensive training programs covering planting techniques, pest and disease control, soil health management, composting and mulching, and post-harvest handling for maximum quality and profitability.

Our Roasting Philosophy

We roast with respect for origin, not just flavor. Every batch is designed to highlight the natural character of Ugandan coffee while meeting specialty-grade standards.

Roast Profiles

  • Light Roast: Floral, fruity, bright acidity
  • Medium Roast: Balanced sweetness, caramel, cocoa
  • Dark Roast: Bold, smoky, chocolate, full-bodied

Quality Control

  • ✓ Small-batch roasting
  • ✓ Sample roasting and cupping
  • ✓ Moisture and density checks
  • ✓ Consistent roast profiling
