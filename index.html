Empowering Ugandan Coffee Farmers, From Seed to Cup

Born in Africa and rooted in community, Kifaru Roasters exists to uplift Ugandan coffee farmers through sustainable farming, ethical trade, and world-class coffee — honoring heritage while building a future of dignity, resilience, and prosperity.

Support a Farmer Buy Coffee

Built on Strength, Driven by Purpose

Kifaru Roasters is a purpose-driven coffee company founded by Kiweewa Farouk, inspired by the strength and dedication of his mother, Nakayima Harriet, and the communities that shaped him. We support farmers with quality seeds, training, market access, and fair pricing — while roasting exceptional coffee that carries the story of Africa to the world.
