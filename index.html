Our story

Built on strength, driven by purpose

Kifaru Roasters was founded by Kiweewa Farouk, inspired by the strength and dedication of his mother, Nakayima Harriet, and the communities that shaped him.

We work alongside smallholder farmers at every stage of the value chain, and we roast exceptional coffee that carries the story of Africa to the world.

  • Quality seedlings and hands-on agronomy training
  • Fair, transparent prices with premiums for quality
  • Small-batch roasting that honours each origin

Our coffees

Two species, one remarkable origin

Uganda is one of very few countries that grows both fine Arabica and world-class Robusta at scale.

Arabica

Highlands
Grown in
Mt. Elgon (Bugisu), Rwenzori, West Nile
Altitude

Process
