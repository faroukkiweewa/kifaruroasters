For farmers

Grow with Kifaru

Are you a coffee farmer or part of a farmer group in Uganda? We offer seedlings, training and a fair market for quality coffee.

  • Certified, disease-resistant Arabica and Robusta seedlings
  • Hands-on training in good farming and post-harvest practices
  • Fair, transparent prices with premiums for quality
  • Group support for drying and processing equipment

When you contact us, please share your name, district and village, farm size, and whether you grow Arabica or Robusta.
