Our Story

Inspired by Strength and Resilience

Kifaru Roasters was founded by Kiweewa Farouk, inspired by the strength, resilience, and unwavering dedication of his mother, Nakayima Harriet. From her tireless work and nurturing spirit came the values that guide us today — hard work, authenticity, dignity, and pride in our roots.

Just as the rhino — Kifaru in Swahili — stands as a symbol of power, protection, and endurance, Nakayima's legacy has become the backbone of our journey. Born in Uganda, Africa, Kifaru Roasters embodies the rich soil, bold spirit, and warm heart of our country.

Every bean we roast tells a story — a story of family, resilience, and the relentless pursuit of excellence by the people who cultivate it.

Why We Started

Solving a simple but powerful problem

Ugandan coffee farmers grow some of the world's finest coffee, yet too many remain underpaid, under-resourced, and disconnected from global markets.

We exist to bridge that gap — by empowering farmers at the source and delivering high-quality coffee to the world with integrity.
